About me

Hi, I'm Ankur.

I studied Electronics and Computer Engineering at Mahindra University and spent a semester at Ecole Centrale de Lille in France. What I enjoy most is the moment a system comes together: a circuit, the firmware running on it, and a machine that finally behaves the way it was designed to.

That has taken me from leading the electrical team of an underwater robot to the SAUVC finals in Singapore, to building SDR links for UAVs at DRDO, to working with ABB and KUKA robots on a production line in France. Along the way I've learned to value careful testing, clear documentation and teaching. I founded the electronics club at Mahindra University, ran workshops for juniors in our AUV club, and still mentor the team today.
